1 Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to StudyLens, created and managed by Sazzidul Corporation. StudyLens is engineered to assist students, teachers, researchers, and lifelong learners in transforming physical books, handwritten study notes, diagrams, and lecture materials into structured revision aids.

Our foundation is built upon a 100% on-device architecture. We believe your notes, ideas, and academic work should remain completely private to your physical device.

2 Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

Unlike conventional study apps that upload your documents to cloud databases, StudyLens operates under strict non-collection rules:

  • No Account Sign-in: You do not need an account, password, phone number, or social media login to use any feature of StudyLens.
  • No Remote Document Storage: Your scanned study notes, camera photos, OCR text extractions, and PDF exports are never transmitted to Sazzidul Corporation servers.
  • No Cloud AI Inference Tracking: Prompts, summaries, and questions are generated locally on your device without sending text to third-party cloud LLMs.

3 Device Permissions & Transparent Usage

StudyLens requests only minimal Android operating system permissions necessary to deliver study functionality:

Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

Used solely when you capture a photo of a textbook page or study document. The camera stream is fed directly into Google ML Kit Text Recognition running locally inside the phone's memory. Camera data is never saved remotely or broadcast.

Microphone Permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

Used solely for the Speech-to-Text audio note feature. Audio is translated into text on-device using Android's native SpeechRecognizer; raw audio recordings are not stored or transmitted to external servers.

Storage & Media Access

Used strictly when you choose to import an existing photo from your gallery or export high-resolution PDF study notes to your device's Downloads or Documents folder.

4 Third-Party Service Providers

StudyLens integrates industry-standard Google SDKs to ensure security and compliance:

  • Google ML Kit (On-Device): Powers rapid text recognition directly on CPU/GPU without cloud communication.
  • Google Play Billing Client: Manages in-app subscriptions and the 1-week free trial securely via Google Play infrastructure.
  • Google AdMob: Serves standard rewarded bonus ads for free-tier users. AdMob processes non-identifiable technical signals (such as device model and advertising ID) in compliance with Google Play Developer policies. Pro subscribers receive an ad-free experience.

5 User Rights, Control & Instant Data Deletion

You have 100% sovereignty over your study notes and preferences:

  • In-App Immediate Erasure: Inside StudyLens Settings, tapping "Delete All Notes & History" permanently wipes your local Room Database (`AppDatabase`) and resets all configuration in real time.
  • App Uninstallation: Because StudyLens stores data exclusively in Android's application-sandboxed directory, uninstalling StudyLens from your device automatically and irreversibly removes all local study databases, notes, and temporary OCR caches.

6 Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA / FERPA)

StudyLens is an educational study tool designed for students of all levels. Because our app collects zero personal identity records, does not request user ages or student school IDs, and does not maintain online databases, we strictly respect and comply with global child protection regulations including COPPA and FERPA standards.
